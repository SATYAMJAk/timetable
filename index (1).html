VFSTR Mass Recruiter Playbook

Your 6-month step-by-step roadmap to secure an offer.

Phase 1 — Master Aptitude

TCS and Wipro eliminate students at the aptitude stage. If you fail Quants or Verbal, your coding skills mean nothing.

  • Quants: Time & Work, Percentages, Speed & Distance, Profit & Loss
  • Logical: Blood relations, Coding-Decoding, Series, Data Sufficiency
  • Action: Solve 30 Qs/day on IndiaBix. Focus on speed.

Phase 2 — Essential Coding

Mass recruiters do NOT ask DP or Graphs. They want clean code for standard array/string problems.

  • Language: Pick Java, C++, or Python and stick to it
  • Topics: Arrays, Strings, Sorting, basic Recursion
  • Action: Striver's A2Z sheet — Easy + Medium only

Phase 3 — Core CS & SQL

Technical interviews heavily focus on CS fundamentals. These questions separate prepared from unprepared students.

  • OOPS: 4 Pillars, Abstraction vs Encapsulation, Overloading vs Overriding
  • DBMS: ACID, Normalization, JOINs (write raw SQL)
  • OS & CN: Paging, Deadlocks, OSI Model, TCP vs UDP

Phase 4 — HR & Resume

The final hurdle. Tests your communication, confidence, and attitude.

  • Projects: Have 2 projects (Web/ML). Explain the architecture clearly.
  • Key Qs: "Tell me about yourself", "5-year goal?", "Willing to relocate?"
  • Bond: TCS/Wipro have 1-year bond. Be prepared to agree.
